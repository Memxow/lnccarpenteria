Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that visited sites send to the user's terminal, where they are stored and the trasmitted back to the same sites on the next visit.

Cookies used by this site

This website uses only Technical and Functionality Cookies. No profiling or advertising tracking cookies are used.

  • Technical and Navigation Cookies: Necessary to ensure the correct functioning of the pages, the dynamic inclusion of components (navbars and footers) and safe navigation.
  • Language Preference Cookies: Used to store the language choice made by the user and keep the selection active while browsing.

The prior consent of users is not required for the installation of such cookies, as they are indispensable to provide in the requested services.

How to disable cookies from the browser

The user can still choose to disable or block cookies by changing the settings of their web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). The deactivation of technical cookies could, however, compromise the correct display of some site feautures.
